I am a PhD researcher at the [European University Institute](https://www.eui.eu/DepartmentsAndCentres/Economics), under the supervision of [Andrea Ichino](https://www.eui.eu/people?id=26493) and [Fabrizia Mealli](https://www.eui.eu/people?id=46800). I am an applied micro-economist with interests in migration and labour.

My research explores how international migration influences natives' perceptions of different immigrant groups in their community, and its impact on the economic and social integration of both earlier and incoming migrants.
